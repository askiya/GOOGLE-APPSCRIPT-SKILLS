# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Give technical feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and never publish credentials or personal data.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers may remove content or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens the safety or health of the community.

This document is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
